## FAQ section

```
Q: Do I need Auto-Tune or any paid plugins?
A: No. The template and all five presets use stock FL Studio plugins only. The
   included demo project uses Auto-Tune on its vocal — that's optional and only
   affects the demo, not the template you'll record into. If you don't own it,
   FL will show a missing-plugin notice on the demo project and everything else
   works normally.

Q: What FL Studio version do I need?
A: 25.2.5 or newer. The pack was built in FL Studio Producer Edition 25.2.5.
   Older versions may not open the project files.

Q: Will this work with my microphone and interface?
A: Yes — the template is interface-agnostic. The guide walks through selecting
   your ASIO driver and choosing the right input, whatever hardware you're on.

Q: Can I release and monetize songs made with this?
A: Yes. Release commercially, monetize on any platform, sell beats or finished
   recordings, and use it on paid client work. No royalties owed.

Q: Can I use it for client sessions?
A: Yes. What you can't do is hand clients the editable template or preset files
   — they'd need their own license. Finished mixes, masters, and stems are fine.

Q: I'm brand new. Is this too advanced?
A: It's built specifically for beginners. The guide assumes no mixer experience
   and covers troubleshooting — no signal, hearing your voice twice, latency,
   crackling, distortion.

Q: Does the vocal chain need adjusting for my voice?
A: Probably a little, and that's normal — every voice, mic, and room is
   different. The guide has a section on what to adjust and when.
```
